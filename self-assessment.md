Professional Self-Assessment – Christina M.

Completing my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science has been a journey spanning approximately six years, starting at Central, continuing at Oakland, and culminating at Southern New Hampshire University. Throughout this time, I have grown not only as a programmer but also as a problem solver, collaborator, and lifelong learner.

Early in my studies, I faced the challenge of balancing structured assignments with creative exploration. For a period, I lost interest in coding because my focus was often on meeting deadlines rather than fully engaging with the learning process. Many assignments had pre-written structures, leaving little room for creative input, which made the work feel more like checking boxes than exploring possibilities. This approach created stress and diminished my enjoyment of learning new programming languages and exploring different areas of computer science.

However, over time, I have regained my passion for coding. Being able to work on projects with creative freedom has allowed me to rediscover the excitement of designing and building solutions on my own terms. In particular, enhancing artifacts for my ePortfolio demonstrated how practical applications of software design, algorithms, and databases can be both technically challenging and personally rewarding. Projects such as improving a 3D graphics system, optimizing algorithms for camera movement and lighting, and designing a user-specific database for an event planning app showcased my ability to think critically, apply industry best practices, and adapt to diverse computing challenges.

Throughout my program, I have honed skills in multiple areas:

Collaboration: Participating in code reviews and group projects taught me how to communicate effectively with peers, provide constructive feedback, and incorporate input to improve software solutions.

Software Engineering and Design: I strengthened my understanding of modular programming, documentation, and code readability while enhancing artifacts to align with professional standards.

Algorithms and Data Structures: I developed the ability to analyze, implement, and optimize algorithms for performance and maintainability in practical applications.

Databases: I gained hands-on experience designing, managing, and manipulating relational databases, applying structured queries, and ensuring data integrity in real-world applications.

Security Awareness: I learned to anticipate potential vulnerabilities, validate inputs, and implement practices to safeguard data and software systems.

Completing this ePortfolio and reflecting on my journey has allowed me to quantify and showcase these skills. It demonstrates not only my technical competency but also my growth in managing projects, solving complex problems, and approaching software development with creativity and strategic thinking. I am proud to present artifacts that reflect both the learning process and the results of applying best practices across multiple domains of computer science.

This experience has reinforced my professional goals: to pursue opportunities where I can continue learning, contribute to meaningful software projects, and engage in creative problem-solving. My journey through the Computer Science program has reminded me that success is not only about completing assignments but about building understanding, confidence, and a genuine passion for the field.
